## Decisions

Settled with the user between 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-14, after the designer rejected v1.

- **A. The approach:** the designer's visual language: Inter; white panels with rounded corners and a soft shadow on #F7F5F2. v1's rules apply on top.
- **B. Contrast:** 7:1 for all text, 3:1 for shapes without text. His hues darkened to pass:
  - action and red text #A31B26 (7.66 with white);
  - muted #58524F, green #10612E, amber #893F07;
  - shapes without text keep his #DC2F3C (4.65);
  - the dashed outline is #8A8480.
- **C. Colour roles** (reopens J4 and Appendix A's avoid 2):
  - red marks the toggle (the primary), the voted card back and the kept frame, and once revealed the most-voted bar(s);
  - faces, figures, links and the focus ring stay ink;
  - Clear is quiet.
- **D. Brand:** his red family over Lunatech burgundy (the same hue, more saturated). lunatech.com's 2026 restyle is not a reference.
- **E. Participants sidebar:**
  - the roster on the right: soft avatars, the name, and the state as a word;
  - facilitators are rows at the bottom;
  - the round history joins the top later.
- **F. Later steps:** only the planned ones are drawn, dashed: the progress, Next issue and the round history. His new ideas (statistics, the "share your reasoning" callout, the round counter, the coffee card) become step 5+ candidates.
- **G. Progress:** the count lives only in the round's place: small in the bar for a voter, big in the slot for a facilitator.
- **H. The issue** is at heading size (1.25 rem) and leaves principle 5's shared-screen list. Its short form is step 9's.
- **I. Avatars:** initials on a pale ground in a hue hashed from the name. They are decorative and `aria-hidden`, and exempt as incidental (J8).
- **J. Result:**
  - v1's 13-column histogram plus a "Most voted" block sized to its content, in a slot of fixed outer size;
  - other bars #E06C74 (3:1);
  - the tie rule (ties step): up to 3 values, then "Tie"; no red bar when every value ties.
- **K, O. The bar:**
  - the phase word (no sentence) and the buttons share their own panel at the bottom of the column;
  - the deck/result panel has no header;
  - layout f, the deck or result above the bar, is the default; g, the deck or result on top, is the open swap.
- **L.** #A31B26 stays for the button and the confirmed card.
- **M. From step 5:** Clear · toggle · Next issue, right-aligned; Next issue is the primary once revealed.
  - No place is kept for Next issue while voting (amends 3b1 decision 6).
  - Step 5 keeps the parent's double-click guarantee; a candidate is J5's 1 s guard extended to Next issue.
  - The candidate "primary chosen by the result" is dropped.
- **N.** The counts above the bars lose their "×".
- **Q. Banners:**
  - full width under the header, each message a panel of its own, with no wrapper panel;
  - problems: #A31B26 on #FEF3F3 with a red bar;
  - notices: #1E3A8A on #EEF4FF with a #1D4ED8 icon and bar.
- **W. Deck cards shrink with the column**, from 5.5 rem tall down to the height whose width is `--target-min` (44 px). The breakpoint is where 13 such cards, their gaps, the paddings and the sidebar fit (about 65.75 rem; Task 1 measures it). The deck is centred in its slot.
- **The header mark:** the Lunatech glyph in white on his red card (#DC2F3C) with its pale offset card; `brand=pip` is the fallback.
- **The app's name stays in the header** (reopens v1 decision 7). "Pointing Poker" is in the URL, so the exact name helps users search for the app. The mark, its colour, the name and any mention of Lunatech can change later in a dedicated branding PR.
